Pre-Registered Disclosures & Negative Controls

What this benchmark does NOT show. Fixed before firing on July 26, 2026. Published in full to survive hostile audit.

1. Rails Do Not Make a Model Omniscient
Rails convert a specific, predictable class of structural failure into a pass. They do not magically grant models knowledge they do not have. Several unstructured capabilities remain off-menu regardless of rails.
2. The 1B Model JSON Rail Negative Control (Turn 04)
When the exact-copy rail was applied to Llama 3.2 1B in Turn 04, the rail broke the JSON output in a novel way by generating excessive escaped quotes. This negative result is reported prominently as required by pre-registration.
3. Frontier Arm Cloud Pinning Limitation
Claude Opus is served over cloud subscription APIs and cannot be pinned to an immutable weight digest the way local models (e.g. llama3.2:3b = a80c4f17acd5) are.
4. Scoring Blinding Disclosure
Scoring was conducted with full visibility into which arm generated the transcript. To eliminate bias, scoring rules were written strictly before firing, and 100% of raw JSONL transcripts are published for third-party auditing.
